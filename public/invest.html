Why year 1 can look negative: at today's rates many East Bay rentals run negative cash flow at first. Rents rise while the loan payment stays fixed, every payment builds equity, and depreciation shelters rental income from tax. The hold-period return counts all of it.

Estimates for comparison only, not tax, legal or financial advice; consult a CPA about your situation. Depreciation is straight-line over 27.5 years on the building share of price, closing costs and rehab (full-year, no mid-month convention). Rental losses use the $25,000 active-participation allowance (phasing out from $100,000 to $150,000 MAGI; California follows the same rule), and losses you can't use are carried forward and released when you sell. At sale, depreciation is taxed (recaptured) federally at up to 25%, the rest of the gain at 15% (20% above about $600,000 of joint income) plus 3.8% net investment income tax when income is over $250,000 joint / $200,000 single; California taxes the whole gain as ordinary income. Refinance costs are paid in cash and not deducted. Growth figures are assumptions, not forecasts. A 1031 exchange can defer taxes at sale.
