Waterfront living

Discovery Bay

A Delta waterfront community where many homes sit on lagoons and channels with private docks. Boating is part of everyday life.

  • Waterfront & docks
  • Delta boating
  • Golf community

Brentwood

Known for its orchards and U-pick farms, with a charming downtown and many newer neighborhoods.

  • U-pick farms
  • Downtown dining
  • Newer neighborhoods
Explore Brentwood on the map →Room to grow

Oakley

A growing East County city near the Delta and Big Break Regional Shoreline, with newer construction and larger lots.

  • Delta access
  • Big Break Shoreline
  • New construction
Explore Oakley on the map →Value on the river

Antioch

A river city on the San Joaquin with BART service, Black Diamond Mines Regional Preserve nearby and a wide range of price points.

  • BART access
  • Riverfront
  • Investor friendly
Explore Antioch on the map →Central & connected

Concord

Central Contra Costa with multiple BART stations, Todos Santos Plaza downtown and Mt. Diablo views.

  • Multiple BART stations
  • Todos Santos Plaza
  • Mt. Diablo views

Walnut Creek

A walkable downtown with shopping and dining, BART access and quick routes to Mt. Diablo State Park trails.
