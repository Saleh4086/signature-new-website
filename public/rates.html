Why might my quote be different?

These are national benchmark averages. Freddie Mac's survey reflects borrowers with strong credit putting 20% down. Your own rate depends on credit score, down payment, loan size and type, points and the lender. Sal can connect you with local lenders for a real quote.

How often is this updated?

The site refreshes from FRED® daily. Freddie Mac publishes weekly on Thursdays, Treasury yields post each business day, and Optimal Blue's indices post daily with a short lag. Every figure shows its own as-of date.

What's the difference between the weekly and daily numbers?

Freddie Mac's PMMS is a weekly survey average. Optimal Blue's Mortgage Market Indices are calculated daily from actual locked rates, so they react faster to market moves.

Where can I follow daily rate commentary?

For day-to-day market commentary, Mortgage News Daily publishes a daily index and analysis. That's an outside site, not affiliated with Blackstone.
